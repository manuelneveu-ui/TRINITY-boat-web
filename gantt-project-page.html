Timeline

  • Semester 1 The first semester includes go / no go validation, ordering electronic components, composite tests, mold work, PLA printing, hull realization and the start of programming.
  • Toussaint break Break planned from 24/10/2025 to 03/11/2025.
  • Winter break Break planned from 19/12/2025 to 02/02/2026.
  • Semester 2 The second semester includes LCA step 3 and 4, final interface work, receiver / transmitter work, foil integration, demonstration, communication material and conclusion.

Main areas

  • Validation and project go / no go steps.
  • Manufacturing, molds and printed parts.
  • Hull realization and boat construction.
  • Programming, interface and control system.
  • LCA, report writing and presentation material.
